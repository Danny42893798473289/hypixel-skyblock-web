## Feature walkthrough

### Economy (Hypixel mirror)
- **Bazaar** — live Hypixel order books refresh every 60s via MarketBot
- **Auction House** — 300 mirrored BIN listings refresh hourly
- Open Bazaar in Hub → pick any commodity → see sync badge + price history chart
- `/api/bazaar/meta`, `/api/auctions?search=hyperion`

### Fishing + Sea Creatures
- Fish at Hub pond, Spider's Den, Park lake, or Crimson shore
- Sea creatures (Squid, Sea Walker, Yeti, etc.) spawn based on Luck of the Sea + Angler gear
- Blue alert banner: "SEA CREATURE! … — press E to fight!"
- Kill for fishing collection items + combat XP

### Garden v2
- Open **Garden** menu from SkyBlock menu (Garden island)
- 24 plot grid: plant/water/harvest via socket events or future UI
- Jacob's Contest: 20-minute contests with bronze/silver/gold medals + coin rewards
- Composter: convert crops → organic matter → composter levels
- Garden visitors buy crops for coins

### Enchant procs
- **Looting** — extra mob coin drops
- **Smite / Bane of Arthropods** — bonus vs undead/spiders
- **Thunderlord / Critical / Giant Killer** — combat procs with floating damage numbers

### Collections
- Tier milestones grant coins + unlock flags (`unlockedRecipes`)
- Toast: "Collection: … (+N coins)"

### Slayers
- RNG meter fills toward guaranteed rare drops (catalysts)
- Existing slayer drop tables wired (Revenant flesh, Tarantula web, etc.)

### Social
- **`/pay <player> <amount>`** — send coins (both must be online)
- **`/trade <player>`** then **`/trade confirm`** — item/coin trade
- **`/visit <player>`** — warp to their private island (+ Social XP)

### Dungeons
- Co-op party syncs shared `dungeonRun` state on join
- Essence drops on floor complete (undead/wither/dragon/gold/diamond)
- Upgrade gear stars with essence + coins

### UI polish
- Floating damage numbers on mob hits (gold, red on crit)
- Sea creature spawn alert
- Auction browse panel: search, sort, pagination
